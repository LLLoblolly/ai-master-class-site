Two Kinds of Learners, One Core Path
🟢

AI-Curious

Older adults or anyone new to technology. They need confidence, safety, simple wins, and repetition.

🔵

Experienced Users

People who already use technology and want to get more capable. They need efficiency, workflows, tools, and automation.

Note

Everyone learns Tracks 0–D as the Core. Students then choose Electives (Health, Money, Family, Business, Creative, Knowledge Management). Advanced students move into the Power User track.
