How to Talk to AI

Five parts of a great prompt — you do not need all five every time.

  1. Role — “Act as a patient teacher.”
  2. Task — “Explain what a deductible is.”
  3. Context — “I am new to this and I am 68.”
  4. Constraints — “Use simple words and no jargon.”
  5. Output — “Give me 5 short bullet points.”

The simple version

Tell AI what you want + give it context + say how you want the answer.

Fix an answer that missed

  • “That is not what I meant. I meant…”
  • “Make it shorter.” / “Explain it more slowly.”
  • “What are you unsure about?”
  • “Show me where this came from.”

Is This a Scam? Quick Checklist

If you can answer “yes” to any one of these, stop and verify.

  • ☐ Is someone rushing me or saying I must act right now?
  • ☐ Am I told to keep it secret from my family or bank?
  • ☐ Do they want payment by gift card, wire, crypto, or a payment app?
  • ☐ Are they asking for a password, login code, or personal numbers?
  • ☐ Is a “relative” calling in trouble and needing money? (Voices can be cloned by AI.)
  • ☐ Did this arrive from someone I did not expect, with a link to click?

What to do

  1. Stop. Hang up or do not reply.
  2. Call back using a number you already trust — not the one they gave you.
  3. Ask family for a private safe word they must say on any emergency call.
  4. Report scams at reportfraud.ftc.gov. For Medicare-related scams, call 1-800-MEDICARE.

Safe to Share? The Traffic Light

Before you type anything into an AI tool, think of the light.

🟢 GREENGeneral questions, recipes, how-to help, explaining a word, planning a trip, writing a thank-you note.
🟡 YELLOWYour first name, your general situation, a letter with names and numbers removed. Leave out details you do not need to share.
🔴 REDSocial Security number, Medicare number, bank or card numbers, passwords, one-time login codes, full date of birth with address, full medical records.

Rule of thumb: if you would not say it out loud in a crowded waiting room, do not type it into an AI.

Phone Basics Quick Card

The moves that unlock everything else.

MoveWhat it does
TapOpen or select. Light and quick.
Long-pressHold a moment to see extra options.
SwipeSlide a finger to scroll or go back.
PinchTwo fingers in or out to zoom.

Make the text bigger

  • iPhone: Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size
  • Android: Settings → Display → Font size (wording varies by phone)

Take a screenshot

  • iPhone with Face ID: press the side button and Volume Up together.
  • Most Android phones: press Power and Volume Down together.

Your phone model may differ — if in doubt, search “screenshot” plus your phone name.

Claude on Your Phone: First 5 Minutes

A calm walkthrough for your very first chat.

  1. Open the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android) and search for Claude.
  2. Install the one made by Anthropic, then open it and sign in.
  3. Tap the message box and type: “Explain what you can help me with, in simple words.”
  4. Try the microphone to talk instead of type.
  5. Try the camera or photo button to ask about a picture (never a card, ID, or private document).
  6. Always double-check important answers.

Check the AI: Is This Answer Right?

AI can sound sure and still be wrong. Run this quick check.

  • ☐ Did I ask it to show its sources? Can I open them?
  • ☐ Does an official site agree? (Medicare.gov, IRS.gov, your bank, your doctor’s office.)
  • ☐ Do the names, dates and numbers check out?
  • ☐ Did I ask, “What are you unsure about?”
  • ☐ For health, legal, tax, money or Medicare decisions — did I confirm with a qualified person?

Remember: AI is a helpful assistant, not the final authority.
